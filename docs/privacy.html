BitPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

BitPulse is a free Android app that shows Bitcoin news, price charts and an AI assistant that explains the market. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and who it is shared with. In short: BitPulse has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell any data.

Information you give us

  • Questions you ask the AI assistant. When you send a message, the app sends it to the BitPulse server together with the last few messages of the same conversation, the current Bitcoin price, recent headlines, and the address of the article you are reading (if any). The server adds that article's text and passes everything to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer. Do not type personal information (names, account numbers, passwords, private keys) into the chat.
  • Reports. If you tap Report under an AI answer, the question, the answer and the article address are sent to the BitPulse server and stored in its log so the developer can review problems with AI answers.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Like most web services, the BitPulse server (hosted on Google Cloud Run) records each request: the time, the address requested, your IP address and the app's user agent. These logs are used to keep the service running and to stop abuse, and are kept for about 30 days.
  • Rate limiting. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per IP address in memory for up to one hour. These counts are not stored.

BitPulse does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers or advertising ID, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves the latest news list and price charts on your phone so it can show them quickly at launch and when you are offline. Chat conversations are not saved; they disappear when you close the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes this data.

Third parties

  • Google (Gemini API and Google Cloud). AI questions are processed by Google's Gemini API, and the server runs on Google Cloud in the European Union (Frankfurt). BitPulse uses the Gemini API's free tier; under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use the content sent to it to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. See the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Data sources. Prices, news and market data come from Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, alternative.me, Blockstream, mempool.space, Cointelegraph, CoinDesk, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) and Bitcoin Optech. Normally the BitPulse server contacts them, so they do not see your IP address; if the server cannot be reached, the app contacts them directly, and they then receive your IP address like any website you visit.
  • Articles you open. Opening an article in your browser takes you to the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Security

All traffic between the app and the BitPulse server is encrypted with HTTPS. The API key for the AI service is kept on the server and never shipped in the app.

Children

BitPulse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can use the news and market features without using the AI assistant. If you would like a report or log entry removed, contact us with the approximate time and we will delete what we can find.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
